About Me

Hi, I'm Lowell!

I enjoy solving complex problems with code, especially in team settings, and I see beauty in simple solutions to difficult challenges. My background in digital art taught me to view complex systems as collections of simpler parts.

This perspective led me to look beyond using tools and toward understanding how they work under the hood, from rendering pipelines to performance-critical systems, and to focus on computer engineering and embedded systems.
